## Q3: Required judgement
```
I had to reject the annual 15% discount fee that was implemented across all enterprise sellers. 
While it is a pricing strategy that can be used later on, human judgement was required that not all enterprise sellers would implement a discount strategy. 
This could have cost great losses for the marketplace if a discount was implemented.
It would have caused a breach of trust where enterprise sellers would perceive the marketplace as being price aggressive. 

The marketplace allowed any enterprise seller to list their solution, which can be detrimental to the success of the marketplace. 
If there is no human intervention, there is no verification process for the seller to list his solution. 
In addition, the business model is flawed because the enterprise seller can be contacted directly from the marketplace. 
This will impeach on the profitability of the marketplace as this will lead customers to contact the sellers out of the marketplace. 
```
